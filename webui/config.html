SkillOpt Self-Evolution Engine

The bot learns from every task you run and improves its own skills in the background. Toggle the settings below and click Save — your values are written to disk and applied immediately.

Auto-loop — background daemon, no manual steps needed

When ON, the plugin periodically harvests rollouts, runs a direct optimizer cycle, and (if auto-adopt is also ON) promotes improvements that pass the validation gate.
OFF by default for safety. First run with this OFF, watch the staged proposals, then turn it ON.

Staged proposals — review, approve, reject, or roll back

Each card is a gated proposal awaiting your decision. Approve promotes it to the live SKILL.md (with a reversible snapshot); Reject records a no; Roll back restores the prior SKILL.md.

Governance — auto opt-in behind a human-approval gate

New skills seen in rollouts are auto-opted-in with require_human_approval: true — they stay pending until you Approve them here. Immutable or opted-out skills are never touched.
